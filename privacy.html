Privacy policy

No data leaves your device.

Skilltrail works entirely offline. Your progress (XP, streak, crowns, deck, gems, settings) is stored only on your phone or in your browser. The app creates no account, asks for no email or name, sends nothing to a server and uses no analytics tool.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): only for the local reminders you enable in your Profile, scheduled on the device, without a server. Vibrations: for answer feedback. Nothing else.

League

League opponents are simulated by the app. No ranking is shared with other people.

Advertising

This version contains no ads and no advertising SDK. If a future version adds some, your consent will be asked first and this page will be updated.

Erase my data

Profile → “Reset my progress”, or uninstall the app: nothing remains anywhere else.

Contact

Through the app’s page on Google Play.
